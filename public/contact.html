Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with Mayin?

Getting started is easy! Simply download our mobile app from the App Store or Google Play Store, create an account, and complete your profile. For brands, you can start creating campaigns immediately. For creators, build your profile and start connecting with brands.

What makes Mayin different from other platforms?

Mayin uses advanced AI technology to verify creator authenticity, ensuring you connect with real influencers who have genuine engagement. Our platform also offers transparent pricing, secure payments, and comprehensive analytics to maximize your campaign ROI.

How does Mayin verify creator authenticity?

Our AI system analyzes multiple data points including engagement patterns, follower growth, content quality, and interaction authenticity. We use machine learning algorithms to detect fake followers and engagement, ensuring only genuine creators are featured on our platform.

What are the fees for using Mayin?

Mayin charges a transparent 10% platform fee on successful campaigns. This covers our AI verification services, secure payment processing, and customer support. There are no hidden fees or subscription costs.

How do I get support if I have an issue?

We offer multiple support channels: in-app chat support, email support at support@mayin.com, and phone support during business hours. Our support team typically responds within 2-4 hours during business days.
