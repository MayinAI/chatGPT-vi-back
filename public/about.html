About Mayin

Revolutionizing influencer marketing by creating authentic connections between brands and creators

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To democratize influencer marketing by providing a transparent, secure, and efficient platform that connects authentic creators with brands that share their values. We believe in building genuine relationships that drive meaningful results for both parties.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted platform for authentic influencer marketing, where every creator can monetize their passion and every brand can find genuine voices to amplify their message.

The Mayin Story

Founded in 2024, Mayin was born from a simple observation: the influencer marketing industry was plagued by fake engagement, inflated metrics, and a lack of transparency that left both brands and creators frustrated.

Our founders, having experienced these challenges firsthand, set out to build a platform that would change the game. They envisioned a world where brands could discover truly authentic creators, and creators could connect with brands that genuinely valued their voice.

Today, Mayin stands as a testament to that vision. We've built a platform that uses cutting-edge AI technology to verify creator authenticity, ensuring that every connection made through our platform is genuine and valuable.
