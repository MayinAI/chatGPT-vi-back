Refund Policy

Understanding our refund policies and processes for the Mayin platform.

Last Updated: January 15, 2024

1. Overview

At Mayin, we strive to provide exceptional service and ensure customer satisfaction. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued and the process for requesting them.

Our Commitment

We believe in fair and transparent refund policies. If you're not satisfied with our service or if we fail to deliver as promised, we'll work with you to resolve the issue and, where appropriate, provide a refund.

2. Refund Eligibility

2.1 When Refunds Are Available

Refunds may be issued in the following circumstances:

Scenario Refund Eligibility Conditions
Service Not Delivered Full Refund If Mayin fails to provide the core service
Technical Issues Partial/Full Refund Extended platform downtime or critical bugs
Campaign Cancellation Pro-rated Refund Before campaign execution begins
Creator Non-Performance Partial Refund If creator fails to deliver as agreed
Billing Errors Full Refund Duplicate charges or incorrect amounts

2.2 When Refunds Are Not Available

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Platform fees for completed campaigns
  • Service charges for account maintenance
  • Campaigns that have been fully executed
  • Violations of our Terms of Service
  • Fraudulent or abusive behavior
  • Third-party payment processor fees

3. Refund Process

How to Request a Refund

1

Contact Support

Reach out to our support team through the app or email

2

Provide Details

Explain the issue and provide relevant documentation

3

Review Process

Our team will investigate and assess your request

4

Resolution

We'll communicate our decision and process the refund if approved

3.1 Required Information

To process your refund request, please provide:

  • Your account information and contact details
  • Transaction ID or payment reference
  • Detailed description of the issue
  • Any relevant screenshots or documentation
  • Preferred refund method

3.2 Response Time

We aim to respond to all refund requests within:

  • Initial Response: 24-48 hours
  • Investigation Period: 3-5 business days
  • Refund Processing: 5-10 business days

4. Campaign-Specific Refunds

4.1 Brand Campaign Refunds

For brand campaigns, refunds are processed based on campaign status:

Campaign Stage Refund Amount Processing Time
Planning Phase 100% of campaign budget 3-5 business days
Creator Selection 90% of campaign budget 5-7 business days
Content Creation 50% of campaign budget 7-10 business days
Published Content No refund available N/A

4.2 Creator Payment Refunds

If a creator fails to deliver as agreed:

  • Brand receives a partial refund based on work completed
  • Creator may receive partial payment for work delivered
  • Platform fees are non-refundable
  • Dispute resolution may be required

5. Refund Methods

5.1 Available Refund Methods

Refunds are processed using the original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: Refunded to the original card
  • Bank Transfers: Refunded to the originating account
  • Digital Wallets: Refunded to the wallet account
  • Platform Credits: Added back to your Mayin account

5.2 Processing Times by Method

  • Credit/Debit Cards: 5-10 business days
  • Bank Transfers: 7-14 business days
  • Digital Wallets: 1-3 business days
  • Platform Credits: Immediate

Important Note

Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider. International refunds may take longer due to currency conversion and banking procedures.

6. Dispute Resolution

6.1 When Disputes Arise

If there's a disagreement about refund eligibility or amounts:

  1. Both parties submit their perspective and evidence
  2. Mayin's dispute resolution team reviews the case
  3. Additional information may be requested
  4. A fair resolution is determined
  5. Both parties are notified of the decision

6.2 Escalation Process

If you're not satisfied with our resolution:

  • Request a review by senior management
  • Provide additional evidence or documentation
  • Consider mediation services (for high-value disputes)
  • Legal action as a last resort

7. Special Circumstances

7.1 Force Majeure Events

In cases of extraordinary circumstances (natural disasters, government actions, etc.):

  • Refund policies may be adjusted
  • Alternative solutions will be offered
  • Communication will be provided promptly
  • Fair compensation will be considered

7.2 Technical Failures

For platform-wide technical issues:

  • Service credits may be offered
  • Extended access periods provided
  • Pro-rated refunds for affected services
  • Compensation for demonstrable losses

8. Preventing Refund Issues

Best Practices

To minimize the need for refunds, we recommend:

8.1 For Brands

  • Clearly define campaign requirements and expectations
  • Review creator profiles and past work thoroughly
  • Communicate promptly with creators
  • Provide clear feedback and guidelines
  • Set realistic timelines and budgets

8.2 For Creators

  • Understand campaign requirements before accepting
  • Communicate any challenges or delays early
  • Deliver content according to agreed specifications
  • Maintain professional communication
  • Meet deadlines and quality standards
